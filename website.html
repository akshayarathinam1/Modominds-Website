Execution

Website Development Process

A proven 5-phase timeline that moves your site from strategy to launch with zero downtime.

Phase 01 2-3 Weeks

Strategy & Planning

Defining sitemaps, user pathways, copywriting outlines, wireframe iterations, and researching competitive advantages to outline structural needs.

Phase 02 3-4 Weeks

High-Fidelity UI/UX Design

Establishing colors, interactive components, responsive page views, checking WCAG contrast accessibility, and confirming custom vector illustrations.

Phase 03 4-6 Weeks

Custom Front-End Engineering

Writing clean, semantics-driven code, configuring content editing dashboards (CMS), setting up contact form routers, and wiring e-commerce gateways.

Phase 04 2-3 Weeks

Testing & Speed Audits

Multi-browser checklist checks, debugging form validations, running Lighthouse performance diagnostics, and optimizing image files for blazing speeds.

Phase 05 1 Week

Secure Launch & Handover

DNS migrations, deploying SSL certificates, provisioning automated system backups, and training your marketing team on managing web page contents.
