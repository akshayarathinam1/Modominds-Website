The Journey

App Development & Submission Process

Our comprehensive 5-phase engineering process from strategy scoping to live App Store release.

01

Product Scoping & Logic Flow

Mapping out interactive user journeys, framing logical views hierarchy layouts, defining data collection architectures, and locking down technical specifications.

02

High-Premium UI/UX Design

Designing modern high-fidelity screens, testing intuitive touch targets, configuring clean micro-animations, and organizing styles to match Apple Human Interface and Material Design standards.

03

Mobile-First Engineering

Writing highly optimized React Native/Flutter codebases, wiring REST/GraphQL APIs, implementing encrypted token authentication, and tuning speed variables.

04

App Store Submission Process

Preparing Apple Developer and Google Play Console environments, auditing guidelines compliance, assembling screenshots portfolios, and managing the entire review submission checklist.

05

Integrating analytics hooks, configuring target keyword metadata (App Store Optimization), establishing dynamic retargeting tags, and planning app index mapping.
